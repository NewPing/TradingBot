## 1. Data Integrity: 100% Real Historical Market Data

### 1.1 Policy: Zero Fabricated / Mock Data
* **Strict Real-Data Mandate:** All backtests, forward paper simulations, stock screening, feature extractions, and research sweeps must execute exclusively on real historical market data. No synthetic or fabricated prices are used in production, backtesting, or research.
* **Role of Mock Data:** Mock data structures exist **only** in isolated unit tests (`tests/unit/`) to verify mathematical boundary conditions (e.g., zero division guards, leap year dates, network error handling). They are strictly quarantined from all execution and analytics engines.

### 1.2 Data Providers & Historical Depth
ATLAS ingests and harmonizes data across four institutional and public APIs:

| Provider | Data Domain | Historical Depth | Update Frequency | Purpose in ATLAS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **yfinance / Yahoo Finance** | EOD OHLCV Bars, Splits, Dividends, Benchmark Indices (`SPY`, `QQQ`, `^GSPC`, `^VIX`) | 20+ Years (2000–Present) | Daily EOD / Fallback | Long-horizon backtesting (10Y/20Y), multi-decade benchmark comparisons, market breadth |
| **Tiingo** | Institutional EOD Adjusted Bars, Corporate Actions, Metadata | 15+ Years (2010–Present) | Daily EOD (Primary) | High-accuracy split/dividend-adjusted price series, historical universe validation |
| **Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)** | Point-in-Time (PIT) Fundamentals: Balance Sheets, Income Statements, Cash Flow, SEC 10-K/10-Q filing dates | 10+ Years (2014–Present) | Quarterly / Annual | L3 Fundamental GARP quality/value screening, eliminating lookahead bias |
| **Alpaca Markets** | Live/Paper Real-Time Feeds, 1m/1h/1d Bars, Financial News Stream | 8+ Years | Real-Time WebSocket & REST | Execution broker, live/paper price streaming, L4 news article feed |

### 1.3 Corporate Actions & Survivorship Bias Prevention
1. **Split & Dividend Adjustment:** Historical bars are adjusted backwards using cumulative adjustment factors ($f_{\text{adj}}$) to guarantee that returns reflect true total shareholder return without artificial price drops on ex-dividend or stock-split dates.
2. **Point-in-Time Universe Snapshots (`universe_snapshots` table):** ATLAS stores historical index membership snapshots to evaluate stocks that were actively listed on each historical date, eliminating survivorship bias (survivorship bias occurs when backtests only evaluate companies that survived until today).

## 2. Multi-Horizon Performance & Benchmark Reporting (10Y, 5Y, 1Y vs. S&P 500)

### 2.1 Multi-Horizon Time Windows
Every strategy run (Backtest, Paper, Live) computes and displays performance across standard institutional horizons:
* **10-Year Long Horizon (10Y):** Evaluates multi-cycle resilience (including bull markets, bear markets, and inflation shocks).
* **5-Year Medium Horizon (5Y):** Evaluates intermediate macroeconomic cycle performance.
* **3-Year Horizon (3Y):** Evaluates medium-term factor momentum.
* **1-Year Short Horizon (1Y):** Evaluates recent trailing performance under current market conditions.
* **Year-to-Date (YTD):** Evaluates calendar year performance.
* **All-Time / Inception-to-Date:** Full backtested lifecycle.

### 2.2 Direct S&P 500 (`SPY`) Index Comparison
For each horizon, ATLAS automatically aligns the strategy equity curve against the benchmark index (`SPY` ETF / S&P 500 Total Return) on identical calendar trading days and calculates:
* **Strategy Total Return (%) & Dollar Earnings ($ PnL):** Clear cash growth (e.g. *Initial $100,000 → Net Profit +$184,250 → Ending Equity $284,250*).
* **Benchmark Total Return (%) & Benchmark Earnings ($):** How a passive Buy-and-Hold SPY investment performed over the exact same period.
* **Alpha ($\alpha$):** Excess return generated above the benchmark on a risk-adjusted basis.
* **Beta ($\beta$):** Strategy sensitivity to broad market moves ($\beta < 1.0$ indicates lower market volatility exposure).
* **Information Ratio & Tracking Error:** Consistency of outperformance versus the benchmark.
* **Drawdown Comparison:** Strategy Maximum Drawdown (%) vs. S&P 500 Maximum Drawdown (%) during the same period.

### 2.3 Comprehensive Performance Metric Table
The following metrics are computed and displayed for every strategy:

| Metric | Plain-English Meaning | Formula / Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate | $\text{CAGR} = \left(\frac{\text{Ending Equity}}{\text{Starting Equity}}\right)^{\frac{252}{N}} - 1$ |
| **Total Net Profit ($)** | Actual money earned | $\text{Net Profit} = \text{Ending Equity} - \text{Starting Capital}$ |
| **Sharpe Ratio** | Return per unit of total risk (annualized) | $\text{Sharpe} = \frac{\bar{R}_{\text{daily}} - R_f}{\sigma_{\text{daily}}} \times \sqrt{252}$ |
| **Sortino Ratio** | Return per unit of *downside* risk (ignores upside volatility) | $\text{Sortino} = \frac{\bar{R}_{\text{daily}} - R_f}{\sigma_{\text{downside}}} \times \sqrt{252}$ |
| **Max Drawdown (MDD)** | Peak-to-trough worst-case portfolio drop | $\text{MDD} = \max_{t} \left( \frac{\text{Peak}_t - \text{Equity}_t}{\text{Peak}_t} \right)$ |
| **Calmar Ratio** | Annual return divided by maximum drawdown | $\text{Calmar} = \frac{\text{CAGR}}{\text{Max Drawdown}}$ |
| **Win Rate (%)** | Percentage of completed round-trip trades with positive PnL | $\text{Win Rate} = \frac{N_{\text{winning trades}}}{N_{\text{total trades}}}$ |
| **Profit Factor** | Gross profits divided by gross losses | $\text{Profit Factor} = \frac{\sum \text{Gross Gains}}{\sum |\text{Gross Losses}|}$ |

### 3.2 AI Systems & Machine Learning Models Used

#### A. LightGBM Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (`atlas/ml/`)
* **What it does:** Predicts the probability of positive forward returns over a 5-day / 21-day holding period using non-linear interactions across 20 technical and statistical features.
* **Lookahead Protection (Purged K-Fold CV):** Models are trained with *Purged K-Fold Cross-Validation* and *Embargo Quarantine Windows* (Lopez de Prado method) to strictly prevent serial correlation and information leakage between training and testing sets.
* **SHAP Local Feature Explainability:** For every stock score, the model outputs **SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations)** values, explaining exactly which factors (e.g. *RSI contributed +0.15, EV/EBITDA contributed +0.08, High Volatility subtracted -0.22*) drove the trade decision.

#### B. 4-Quadrant Macro Regime Detector (`atlas/signals/regime.py`)
* **What it does:** Analyzes broad market conditions (`SPY` benchmark) to adjust strategy risk dynamically.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  1. *Trend Condition:* $\text{Trend} = \text{Close}_{\text{SPY}} > \text{SMA}_{200}(\text{SPY})$ (Bullish if True, Bearish if False).
  2. *Volatility Condition:* $\sigma_{20} = \text{StdDev}(\text{Daily Returns}, 20) \times \sqrt{252}$. Compared against the 2-year rolling median ($\text{Median}(\sigma_{504})$).
  3. *Regimes:*
     * `BULL_LOW_VOL`: Optimal trending market $\rightarrow$ 100% position sizing, aggressive momentum.
     * `BULL_HIGH_VOL`: Choppy bull market $\rightarrow$ 70% position sizing, tighter trailing stops.
     * `BEAR_LOW_VOL`: Slow grind down $\rightarrow$ 30% position sizing, selective value.
     * `BEAR_HIGH_VOL`: Panic / Crisis selloff $\rightarrow$ 0% long exposure, capital moved 100% to cash or defensive hedges.

#### C. Narrative & LLM Intelligence (`atlas/llm/`)
* **What it does:** Ingests breaking news articles, press releases, and SEC filings.
* **LLM Engine:** Connects to local LLMs (Qwen/Llama) or cloud models (Claude/OpenAI) using structured JSON output contracts.
* **Evaluation Attributes:**
  * `sentiment_score`: Float between $-1.0$ (disastrous/litigation/fraud) and $+1.0$ (transformative earnings/FDA approval).
  * `impact_horizon`: `SHORT` (1–3 days), `MEDIUM` (1–4 weeks), `LONG` (1–12 months).
  * `rationale`: Plain-English explanation generated by the LLM summarizing the core fundamental driver.

### 3.3 Core Mathematical & Indicator Formulas

1. **Cross-Sectional 12-1 Month Momentum:**
   $$\text{MOM}_{12-1}(i) = \frac{P_{t-21}(i)}{P_{t-252}(i)} - 1$$
   *Measures the 12-month return skipping the most recent 1 month (to avoid short-term reversal noise).*

2. **Relative Strength Index (RSI):**
   $$\text{RSI} = 100 - \left(\frac{100}{1 + \frac{\text{EMA}(\text{Gains}, N)}{\text{EMA}(\text{Losses}, N)}}\right)$$

3. **Average True Range (ATR) & Volatility-Adjusted Trailing Stop:**
   $$\text{TR}_t = \max\left(H_t - L_t, |H_t - C_{t-1}|, |L_t - C_{t-1}|\right), \quad \text{ATR}_N = \text{EMA}(\text{TR}, N)$$
   $$\text{Stop Loss Price} = \text{Peak Price} - \left(3.0 \times \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$

4. **Sloan Accrual Ratio (Earnings Quality):**
   $$\text{Accrual Ratio} = \frac{\text{Net Income} - \text{Operating Cash Flow}}{\text{Total Assets}}$$
   *Values $> 0.05$ indicate that earnings are driven by non-cash accounting accruals rather than real cash flow, flagging a high risk of future earnings downgrades.*

5. **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC):**
   $$\text{ROIC} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Invested Capital}} = \frac{\text{Operating Income} \times (1 - \text{Tax Rate})}{\text{Total Debt} + \text{Total Equity} - \text{Cash}}$$

6. **Inverse-Volatility Position Sizing:**
   $$w_i = \frac{\frac{1}{\sigma_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^N \frac{1}{\sigma_j}}, \quad \text{Capital Allocation}_i = \text{Bucket Cash} \times w_i$$
   *Allocates more capital to stable stocks and less capital to highly volatile stocks, equalizing risk contribution.*

## 4. Algorithmic Universe Selection & Dynamic Multi-Phase Subsystems

### 4.1 How Stocks Are Evaluated & Selected (Is Nvidia always traded?)
* **No hardcoded stocks:** NVIDIA (NVDA), Apple (AAPL), or any specific stock is **never permanently fixed** in the trading universe.
* **Algorithmic Universe Selection:** Stocks are dynamically screened on each date using Point-in-Time criteria:
  1. **Liquidity Filter:** Minimum Average Daily Dollar Volume ($\text{ADV}_{20} = \text{SMA}_{20}(\text{Close} \times \text{Volume}) \ge \$20{,}000{,}000$) ensures positions can be entered and exited with negligible market impact.
  2. **Price Filter:** Minimum Share Price $\ge \$5.00$ excludes penny stocks and illiquid micro-caps.
  3. **Point-in-Time Index Membership:** Considers whether the stock was an active constituent of the S&P 500 / Russell 1000 at that historical point in time.

A strategy can execute across distinct scheduled phases:

## 6. Autonomous Research Loop & Removal of the 500 Trial Limit

### 6.1 Removal & Uncapping of the 500 Research Limit
* **Why the 500 limit existed:** The `500` limit was an internal software constant (`weekly_trial_budget = 500`) initially designed to illustrate weekly testing budget tracking. **It is NOT imposed by any external API or provider.**
* **v1.5 Upgrade:** 
  1. The artificial 500 cap is removed.
  2. Research sweeps and hypothesis discovery can run **unlimited** or with a user-configurable limit via `.env` (`ATLAS_TRIAL_BUDGET=unlimited` or custom integer) and directly via the UI / CLI.
  3. Multiple-testing statistical corrections (Deflated Sharpe Ratio) scale mathematically to any number of trials ($N = 10{,}000+$) without requiring an arbitrary hard stop.

### 6.2 The Self-Improving Strategy Discovery Loop
The **`ResearchDaemon`** autonomously explores, optimizes, and discovers new strategies without human bias:

1. **Hypothesis Generation Modalities:**
   * **Parameter Refinement:** Explores parameter neighborhoods around winning strategies (e.g. testing SMA periods 180–220, RSI thresholds 8–15).
   * **Feature Combination:** Automatically tests adding L2/L3/L4 signals to baseline L1 strategies.
   * **Regime Conditioning:** Adds macro regime filters to reduce drawdowns during historical bear markets (e.g. 2008 GFC, 2020 Covid crash, 2022 rate hikes).
   * **Genetic Crossover:** Crosses over high-performing components from two different strategy families (e.g., combining the entry signal of Strategy A with the risk management of Strategy B).
2. **The 8 Econometric Promotion Gates (§8.3):**
   Candidate strategies must pass 8 rigorous statistical tests to be promoted to the Human Review Queue:
   * **Gate 1 (Walk-Forward):** $\ge 6$ rolling OOS folds with median fold Sharpe $\ge 0.50$ and $\le 1$ negative fold.
   * **Gate 2 (Parameter Stability):** Strategy Sharpe must not degrade by $> 40\%$ when parameters are perturbed by $\pm 20\%$.
   * **Gate 3 (Monte Carlo Permutation):** 1,000 bootstrap trade sequence shuffles; 5th percentile CAGR must remain $> 0\%$.
   * **Gate 4 (Cost Stress Test):** Must remain profitable when slippage and commissions are doubled ($2.0\times$ cost model).
   * **Gate 5 (Regime Breakdown):** Maximum drawdown during historical bear regimes must not exceed $25\%$.
   * **Gate 6 (Sample Size):** Minimum $\ge 100$ completed round-trip trades.
   * **Gate 7 (Overfitting Guard):** Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) $p\text{-value} \ge 0.95$ and CSCV Probability of Backtest Overfitting ($\text{PBO}) \le 0.30$.
   * **Gate 8 (Correlation Guard):** Daily return correlation with existing active strategies must be $< 0.85$ (guaranteeing true portfolio diversification).
